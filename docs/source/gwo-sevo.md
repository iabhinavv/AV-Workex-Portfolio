# Affiliate and creator network

Start with the merchant’s objective, build a suitable partner shortlist and make the delivery measurable. The working file should connect the first commercial conversation to campaign decisions, reporting and follow-up.

## Define the offer

Agree the audience, geography, business objective and delivery constraints. Decide whether the engagement is a performance affiliate programme, managed creator campaign or an agreed combination. Set the payable event or deliverable, responsibilities, budget, timing and success criteria before proposing a pilot.

**Output:** A brief that the sales and delivery teams can both explain, with clear commercial terms and an approval route.

## Build the merchant pipeline

1. Preserve existing records and use stable merchant IDs. Prioritise businesses with a relevant audience, viable offer, fulfilment capacity and a measurable objective.
2. Research the business and appropriate contact route. Keep the current stage, owner, next action and follow-up date on every active opportunity.
3. Use discovery to qualify the need, decision process and delivery requirements. Tailor the proposal and follow-up to that conversation.
4. Keep declined, deferred and unresponsive prospects distinct. Move to an active campaign only after commercial approval.

**Pipeline:** Research, outreach, discovery, qualified brief, proposal, commercial review, pilot approval, active campaign, renewal or close.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Discovery and follow-up prompts</summary>

- What outcome matters now: sales, leads, awareness, content or another action?
- Who is the audience, where are they and what offer will they see?
- What has already been tried, and what needs to improve?
- What budget, acquisition-cost or fulfilment constraints affect the plan?
- Who approves the proposal and supports delivery, tracking and reporting?
- What would make a pilot useful, and what is the next agreed action?

Follow up with the buyer’s need, the relevant service, the missing information and one specific next step. Send a selected portfolio when it is more useful than a general deck.

</details>

## Build and match the creator roster

Maintain stable creator IDs and separate platform accounts. Verify identity, handles, profile links, categories, recent content and dated audience information. Track scouting, contact, verification, contract and availability separately.

Translate the brief into selection criteria before choosing names. Review direct specialists first, then relevant adjacent creators. Check audience fit, communication style, availability, pricing, work samples and brand suitability; prepare backups.

**Output:** A ranked shortlist with a reason for each recommendation and any open question that affects booking.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Creator evidence and selection checks</summary>

- Match each account, image and supporting record to the correct person.
- Record platform-specific metrics with their meaning and capture date. Keep missing data distinct from zero.
- Do not multiply a creator-level total across platforms or describe combined followers as unique people without a valid method.
- Use typical recent content to assess delivery; exceptional views alone do not establish expected performance.
- Confirm current analytics, permissions, rates, availability and campaign suitability before booking.
- Mark recommendations consistently: direct fit, adjacent fit, lower fit or needs review.

</details>

## Prepare portfolios and onboarding files

Select the presentation for its recipient: a broad directory, limited prospect preview or campaign-specific shortlist. Show relevant creators and only information approved for that audience. Test filters, detail views and enquiry links on phone and desktop.

For onboarding, map the master roster to the receiving system’s current template. Preserve IDs, supported accounts and image references. Test a representative batch, inspect the accepted records and reconcile rejections before the full transfer.

**Review point:** A hidden field or blurred image does not restrict access. Exclude private roster information from public files.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Data hand-off checklist</summary>

- Keep a backup, field map and current valid example.
- Match required fields and data types; validate lists and structured values.
- Count creators and platform accounts separately. Reconcile transferred records, exclusions and duplicates.
- Verify that image references exist and belong to the intended creator.
- Resolve missing linking fields and rejected rows; retry only the corrected records.
- Inspect records in the receiving system and retain a rollback route.
- Check standalone portfolios for missing assets, broken links and unintended private data.

</details>

## Deliver and measure campaigns

Turn the approved brief into partner instructions, content requirements, tracking links, launch checks and a reporting calendar. Confirm owners, approval contacts and the process for changes or exceptions before launch.

Reconcile results to the merchant’s confirmed actions and contractual rules. Keep pending, approved, reversed and disputed activity separate. Review channel trends, explain variances and record the decision behind each change.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Dashboard definitions and review checks</summary>

| Measure | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Attributed sales | Merchant sales associated with campaign activity |
| Commission | Recorded commission, checked for approval and reversals |
| Channel cost | Media or messaging costs included in the reporting inputs |
| Campaign contribution | Commission less included direct costs; not full-company net profit |

Recompute totals from dated inputs and reconcile sample dates and channels. Keep the selected channel, period and currency consistent across charts, totals and exports. Document the exchange rate and conversion direction when currencies differ.

Check missing values, true zero, partial periods, duplicate dates, invalid inputs, negative contribution and empty filters. Use matching totals for ratios; leave a ratio unavailable when its denominator is zero or missing. Preserve the previous valid report when an import fails.

</details>

## Keep the team aligned

| Rhythm | Review | Result |
|---|---|---|
| During delivery | Launch readiness, tracking, approvals and urgent issues | Clear ownership and resolved blockers |
| Weekly | Pipeline, follow-ups, creator availability and campaign performance | Updated priorities and next actions |
| Settlement cycle | Approved results, commission, invoices, collections and payouts | Reconciled settlement and renewal decisions |

Maintain the merchant pipeline, campaign brief, partner register, launch checklist and reporting/settlement file. Give every open action an owner and due date. Limit access to commercial terms, personal data and private roster information.
